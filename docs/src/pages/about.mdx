# Behind wp-react

Hi, I'm **Farahmand Moslemi** — the creator and maintainer of `wp-react`.

## My Background

I've spent years working across the full stack, with deep roots in the CMS and e-commerce ecosystem — including **OpenCart**, **WordPress**, **Joomla**, **Magento**, and **Symfony**. That long history with PHP-based platforms gave me a strong intuition for how developers actually build real-world sites on top of these systems.

On the frontend side, I work with modern frameworks and tooling: **React**, **Angular**, **Vanilla JS**, and styling systems like **Tailwind CSS**, **SASS**, and **Bootstrap**. I also enjoy bringing interfaces to life with **GSAP**, **Three.js**, and **D3** when the project calls for it.

Beyond web development, I stay curious about systems programming, game development, and data work — I'm familiar with **Rust**, **Python**, **C++**, **Java**, **Node.js (Express)**, **MongoDB**, **MySQL**, and even experimenting with **Godot Engine** from time to time.

## Why wp-react?

`wp-react` was born from the intersection of these two worlds. Having built so many WordPress-based projects, I kept rewriting the same API glue code, wrestling with untyped REST responses, and bolting on auth flows. At the same time, my love for modern React and TypeScript made the WordPress developer experience feel dated.

The library is my attempt to bring the two halves together: the CMS ecosystem I've known for years, with the type-safe, hook-based DX that modern frontend developers expect.
